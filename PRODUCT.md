# ElAgente Workbench

## Register

product

## Users

Scientists using Grafico, Quntur and Estructural to read scientific conversations,
inspect calculations and molecular structures, and answer workflow questions on
desktop and mobile. Grafico includes its Optima and Potente workflows.

## Product Purpose

Keep scientific conversation, files, immutable results and interactive structures
together. Shared components consume host-provided DTOs and callbacks. Scientific
execution, authentication, AIS transport and billing belong to the host/backend.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, trustworthy. Preserve the existing workbench and offer Terminal as
an optional appearance, independently of light/dark mode.

## Anti-references

No anime themes, coding-account settings, device management, oversized task banners,
or controls that suggest unsupported coding capabilities. Do not rewrite scientific
answers or represent scientific jobs as shell commands.

## Design Principles

- Keep authored text, formulas, units and scientific evidence intact.
- Preserve scientific viewer identity, camera and frame state during updates.
- Make routine activity secondary and reveal details on demand.
- Keep phone actions reachable and menus stable under touch.
- Keep preferences local and separate from Agent/model configuration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast,
reduced-motion support and usable touch targets. Theme changes must preserve the
meaning of scientific states and avoid recoloring molecular canvases.
